Garuda Glory Team Garuda (30 students from 2nd, 3rd, and 4th year Mechanical Engineering) from Rajiv Gandhi University of Knowledge Technologies (RGUKT), Basar has achieved remarkable success by participating in two prestigious national-level competitions: M-BAJA SAEINDIA 2026 held at BVRIT, Narsapur, Telangana, and ATVC INDIA 2026 held at Monark University, Ahmedabad. 1. Performance at M-BAJA SAEINDIA 2026 (BVRIT, Narsapur, Telangana) (25th February – 3rd March 2026 - Total 55 Teams Participated) Team Garuda secured an outstanding All India Rank (AIR) 3rd in the Design Engineering Category, marking a significant milestone for the institution. 2. Performance at ATVC INDIA 2026 (Monark University, Ahmedabad) (27th – 31st March 2026 - Total 43 Teams Participated) Team Garuda further continued its success by delivering an exceptional performance at ATVC INDIA 2026.
