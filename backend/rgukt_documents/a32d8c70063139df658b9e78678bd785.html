Events The PPR section is responsible for organizing Institute level functions such as the Convocation, Foundation Day, Presentation Ceremony of Awards, Alumni Day, Teachers’ Day and the institute colloquiums, to mention prominent ones. The department also coordinates visits of high profile national and international visitors and delegations, including Heads of States, Members of Assembly/Parliament and other special guests. Moreover, we provide assistance to internal departments for organizing their events.
