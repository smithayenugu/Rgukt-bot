The Department is well facilitated with 11 laboratories wrapping in modern state of the art infrastructure with committed and dedicated Lab Staff, who are always available to assist the student needs and to maintain the Laboratories. The Department encourages the participation of Faculties in various conferences, research papers with active involvement in R&D activities.
