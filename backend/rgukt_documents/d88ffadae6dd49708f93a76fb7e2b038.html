Racing Excellence Team GARUDA has achieved a proud milestone by securing All India Rank (AIR) 5 in ISNEE Season 13, competing against teams from across the country. The team delivered strong performances in several events securing, AIR 1 in the Acceleration event with a record timing of 5.46 seconds, AIR 2 in the Design Quiz, AIR 3 in the Engineering Design Review, AIR 5 in the Disassembly and Assembly event, AIR 6 in the Autocross event AIR 5 in the Overall Performance in the event The team maintained consistent results in other dynamic events including Autocross and Endurance.
